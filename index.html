Our Services

Four services, one app

Everything a farmer needs between spotting a problem in the field and solving it — without a single trip to town.

01
నిపుణుల సలహాEXPERT CROP ADVISORY

Speak your problem, type it, or send photos of the affected crop. A certified agronomist reviews it with your crop, stage and plot area in hand — and replies with a written recommendation, not a guess.

02
సరైన మందు, సరైన మోతాదుRIGHT INPUT, RIGHT DOSE

Every product page states the dose per acre and per tank, how to apply it, what problem it solves, which crops it suits, and the safety precautions to take. No dealer guesswork, no over-spraying.

03
ఇంటి వద్దకే డెలివరీDELIVERY TO YOUR DOOR

Order from the app, pick your address, pay by UPI, net banking or cash on delivery. Track the order, confirm it on arrival, and download a proper GST invoice for your records.

04
ఫీల్డ్ సహాయం & FPO భాగస్వామ్యంFIELD & FPO SUPPORT

Field officers visit villages, help farmers register, and follow up after every purchase. FPOs and FPCs get their own portal to manage member farmers, raise procurement requests and track advisories.

The Farmer's Path

From "ఏమైంది నా పంటకు?" to a solved problem

Seven steps. Each one is a screen a farmer actually taps — nothing hidden, nothing that needs a middleman.

1
నమోదు చేసుకోండిRegister once

A mobile number and an OTP is all it takes. Can't read the OTP? The app reads it aloud. Register as an individual farmer, or as a member of your FPO/FPC — you get a Farmer ID either way.

🎙️ Voice OTP📧 Email OTP backup🆔 Farmer ID
2
మీ పంటలు జోడించండిAdd your crops

Pick your crop in Telugu or English, enter acreage — presets or exact decimals — and the season. Your crop history stays in one place, so advice always arrives with the full picture of your land.

🌾 Crop & stage📐 Acres or cents📚 Crop history
3
నిపుణుడిని అడగండిAsk an expert

Press and speak, or type, or attach photos of the affected leaves. Choose a category, confirm the plot area, and send. No literacy barrier, no waiting at a dealer's counter.

🎙️ Voice question📷 Multiple photos🗂️ Ticket category
4
సలహా అందుకోండిGet the recommendation

A certified agronomist reviews the ticket and replies within 24 hours: what the problem is, which product to use, the dosage, the application method, and the safety precautions — written down, in Telugu, saved in your app.

⏱️ 24-hour response💊 Dose per acre⚠️ Safety notes
5
సరైన ఉత్పత్తి కొనండిOrder the right product

The recommended product is one tap away. The page shows key benefits, suitable crops, the problem it solves, dose per tank, how to apply it, expiry and batch — and honest pricing with GST shown separately. Out of stock? Alternatives are listed.

🧾 Transparent pricing🔁 Alternatives shown⬇️ Download info
6
ఇంటికి డెలివరీDelivered and tracked

Save your delivery address, pay by UPI, net banking or cash on delivery, then follow the order on a live tracker. Confirm delivery on arrival and download the GST invoice straight from the app.

📍 Saved addresses📦 Live tracking🧾 GST invoice
7
అభిప్రాయం ఇవ్వండిTell us if it worked

Rate the advice and the product, and say plainly whether it helped — అవును 👍 or లేదు 👎. A field officer follows up in person, and the feedback goes straight back to the agronomist who advised you.

⭐ Rate the advice🔄 Field follow-up📈 Advice improves

Why It Helps

What actually changes in the field

Not features — outcomes. This is the difference a farmer notices in the first season.

🎯

No more guessing the dose

Advice comes with the exact quantity for your plot size, so you stop spraying more than the crop needs — and stop paying for it.

సరైన మోతాదు, ప్రతిసారీ.
🗣️

Advice in your own words

Ask in Telugu, by voice, the way you'd ask a neighbour. Read the answer in Telugu too. Language stops being the thing that keeps expertise out of reach.

మీ భాషలోనే సలహా.
🧑‍🔬

A real agronomist, not a shop counter

Every recommendation is written by a certified agronomist who can see your crop, stage and photos — and whose advice you get to rate afterwards.

నిపుణుల చేతిలో మీ ప్రశ్న.
🛡️

Safety said out loud

Protective gear, waiting periods and handling precautions appear with every recommendation and every product — not buried on the back of a bottle.

భద్రత ముందు.
📶

Works where the signal doesn't

Screens are built light, status is always visible, and nothing is lost when the network drops mid-village. Voice OTP covers the days SMS won't arrive.

తక్కువ నెట్‌వర్క్‌లోనూ పనిచేస్తుంది.
🧾

A written record of everything

Crop history, past advice, every order and every invoice stay in the app — useful at the bank, useful with your FPO, useful next season.
